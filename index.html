General Features & Attachment 01

  • Bone Type: Flat triangular bone located on the anterolateral wall of the thorax.
  • Attachment to Thorax: No bony articulation with the thoracic cage; attached purely by muscles (Synsarcosis).
  • Joint Formation: Articulates distally with the Humerus to form the Shoulder Joint.
Clinical Note: Shoulder joint lacks true collateral ligaments and is functionally stabilized by surrounding musculature.
Equine Scapula - Lateral Aspect

Spine of Scapula & Acromion Status 02

  • Spine of Scapula (Spina Scapulae): Prominent ridge crossing the lateral surface.
  • Tuber Spinae: Thickened prominence located near the middle of the spine.
  • Acromion Process: ABSENT in Horse; the spine fades distally.
  • Nutrient Foramen: Located in the distal third near the caudal border or medial aspect.
Spina Scapulae & Tuber Spinae

Lateral Surface & Fossae Ratio 03

  • The Spine divides the Facies Lateralis into two unequal fossae:
  • Supraspinous Fossa: Smaller cranial fossa.
  • Infraspinous Fossa: Larger caudal fossa.
  • Anatomical Ratio: Supraspinous to Infraspinous ratio in horse is approximately 1 : 2.
Fossae Ratio (1 : 2)

Medial Surface & Facies Serrata 04

  • Medial Surface (Facies Medialis): Costal aspect facing the thoracic wall.
  • Subscapular Fossa: Wide concave area occupying middle & lower medial surface.
  • Facies Serrata:
    • Two rough triangular areas at the proximal angles.
    • Insertion site for Serratus Ventralis Muscle.
Medial View - Facies Serrata

Glenoid Cavity & Extremity 05

  • Glenoid Cavity: Oval articular concavity for Head of Humerus.
  • Glenoid Notch: Distinct notch on the anteromedial border of glenoid cavity.
  • Supraglenoid Tubercle: Large prominent tubercle anterior to glenoid cavity for Biceps Brachii origin.
  • Coracoid Process: Small tubercle on medial side of Supraglenoid tubercle.
Glenoid Cavity & Supraglenoid Tubercle

Summary of Equine Scapular Features 06

  • Triangular Shape: Flat bone attached via Synsarcosis.
  • Acromion Process: ABSENT in horses.
  • Tuber Spinae: Well-developed central tuberosity on spine.
  • Fossae Ratio: Supraspinous to Infraspinous is 1 : 2.
  • Facies Serrata: Two triangular rough areas proximally.
  • Glenoid Notch: Present on anteromedial margin of Glenoid Cavity.
